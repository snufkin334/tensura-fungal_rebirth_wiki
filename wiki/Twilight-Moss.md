# Twilight Moss

![Twilight Moss icon](link_da_imagem)

> *"You've become a being of fungal duality that spreads clouds of twilight-moss. This living network blesses and protects your allies while aggressively weakening and consuming enemies."*

**Twilight Moss** is the Intrinsic Skill of the [[Myco-Lich Luminary]], the fourth phase of the [[Fungal Lineage]]. It surrounds the user with a living cloud of moss, half light and half darkness, that blesses allies and slowly consumes enemies.

## Activation

Place the skill on Tensura's **ability bar** and press its key to **activate** it. Press it again while it is active to **turn it off** early, at no cost.

---

## The Moss Area

While active, a **sphere** of moss surrounds you, centered at the middle of your body. It is drawn with two kinds of particles: near-white with a hint of yellow, and near-black with a hint of red.

As you move, you leave a **trail**: points along your path keep an identical sphere of moss for **1.5 seconds**.

| Area | Normal | Mastered |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | 60 seconds | 80 seconds |
| Radius | 10 blocks | 15 blocks |
| Trail lifetime | 1.5 seconds | 1.5 seconds |

The skill's state is saved on the player, so the remaining duration continues if you log out and back in.

---

## Effects

Every **2 seconds**, each creature inside your moss sphere **or** inside any active trail is affected. The 2-second timer is counted separately for each creature.

| Target | Normal | Mastered |
|---|---|---|
| **Enemies** | Wither I + Slowness I + **10** Darkness damage | Wither II + Slowness II + **20** Darkness damage |
| **Allies** | Regeneration I + Speed I | Regeneration II + Speed II |

- **Enemies** are any living creatures that are not your allies, including monsters, animals and other players.
- **Allies** are your teammates, Tensura allies and subordinates, your pets and your [[Spore Puppeteer]] puppets.
- **You do not receive the ally buffs yourself.**
- The damage is Tensura's **Darkness** elemental damage.
- The status effects last 3 seconds, so they stay active as long as the target remains in the moss.

## Cost and Cooldown

| | Normal | Mastered |
|---|---|---|
| Magicule cost | 30,000 | 40,000 |
| When it is paid | On activation, from your current Magicules | Same |
| Cooldown | 80 seconds | 60 seconds |
| When the cooldown starts | When the skill ends (turned off or expired) | Same |

No cooldown runs while the skill is active. Turning it off early makes the cooldown start sooner.

> **Note:** The mastered version costs **more** Magicules, but it lasts longer, covers a larger area, deals double damage and recharges faster.

## Notes

- Moving around a fight spreads the moss further, since the trail keeps hitting targets where you just were.
- Twilight Moss pairs well with puppets from [[Spore Puppeteer]], which receive Regeneration and Speed, and with [[Mycelial Reprisal]], since enemies hitting you while you are rooted keep taking Darkness damage.
