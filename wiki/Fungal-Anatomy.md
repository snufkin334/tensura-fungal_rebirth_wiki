# Fungal Anatomy

> *"Your sprout-like body provides intrinsic benefits. Gain a permanent minor speed boost, continuous Night Vision, and spongy shock absorption for falls up to 10 blocks."*

**Fungal Anatomy** is the Intrinsic Skill of the [Myconid Sprout](Myconid-Sprout.md), the first phase of the [Fungal Lineage](Fungal-Lineage.md). It represents the light, spongy body of a mushroom sprout: faster on its feet, able to see in the dark and able to absorb long falls.

## Activation

Fungal Anatomy is a **toggle** skill. It can be turned on or off in two ways:

- Check or uncheck its box in the **skill menu**.
- Place it on Tensura's **ability bar** and press its key. A cast sound plays when it turns on, and an uncast sound when it turns off.

It has no cost of any kind, so it can stay on all the time.

---

## Effects

While the skill is on:

| Effect | Normal | Mastered |
|---|---|---|
| Movement Speed | +15% of the race's base speed | +25% of the race's base speed |
| Safe fall distance | No fall damage from falls of up to **10 blocks** | No fall damage from falls of up to **15 blocks** |
| Night Vision | Permanent | Permanent |

- The speed bonus is applied on top of the race's own speed multiplier.
- The safe fall distance is the total: a normal player already takes no damage from falls of up to 3 blocks, and the skill raises that limit to 10 (or 15).
- The Night Vision effect has **infinite duration**, so it never flickers or runs out. It has no particles and no icon.

All effects are checked once per second. If you die or drink milk, they are restored automatically while the skill is on.

---

## Mastery

| Mastery | Value |
|---|---|
| Gain | +1 mastery point every **30 seconds** while the skill is on |
| Time to master | About 50 minutes of active use (with Tensura's default maximum of 100) |

The mastery gain is affected by the player's mastery gain bonus from Tensura.

When mastered, the speed bonus rises from **+15% to +25%** and the safe fall distance from **10 to 15 blocks**. The upgrade is applied automatically, without turning the skill off and on.

---

## Notes

- Turning the skill off removes only its own Night Vision. A Night Vision potion you drank is not removed.
- If the skill is forgotten while it is on, all of its bonuses are removed.
- Fungal Anatomy is kept when evolving, so every later phase can still use it.
