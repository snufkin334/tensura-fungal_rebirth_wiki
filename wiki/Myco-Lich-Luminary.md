# Myco-Lich Luminary

> *"A bioluminescent mushroom lich, suspended between light and darkness."*

The **Myco-Lich Luminary** is the fourth phase of the [Fungal Lineage](Fungal-Lineage.md). Having mastered the arts of magic, the dreadnought sheds its heavy armor and becomes a glowing mushroom lich, a being of both light and darkness. It spreads clouds of twilight moss that protect its allies and slowly consume its enemies.

## Obtaining

Evolve from a [Mycelial Dreadnought](Mycelial-Dreadnought.md). Each requirement counts for 50% of the evolution progress.

| Requirement | Amount | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Existence Points (EP) | 400,000 | 50% |
| Master **Aspectual**, **Spiritual** or **Summoning** spells | 6 | 50% |

### About the mastered spells requirement

- Every spell of those three types that you have **mastered** counts, including spells mastered before you became a Mycelial Dreadnought. Nothing is reset when the requirement appears.
- **Miscellaneous** spells do not count.
- If you **forget** a spell, it stops counting. Relearning it resets its mastery.
- The evolution screen shows your progress as *X/6*.

### Twilight Moss

> *"You've become a being of fungal duality that spreads clouds of twilight-moss. This living network blesses and protects your allies while aggressively weakening and consuming enemies."*

Twilight Moss is an active skill that must be placed on Tensura's ability bar. Press it once to **activate** it; press it again while it is active to **turn it off early** (at no cost).

While active, a sphere of twilight moss surrounds you, made of near-white particles (with a hint of yellow) and near-black particles (with a hint of red). As you move, you leave a **trail**: every point you pass keeps an identical area of moss for **1.5 seconds**.

| Area | Normal | Mastered |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | 60 seconds | 80 seconds |
| Radius | 10 blocks | 15 blocks |
| Trail lifetime | 1.5 seconds | 1.5 seconds |

Every **2 seconds**, each creature inside the moss sphere or inside any active trail is affected:

| Target | Normal | Mastered |
|---|---|---|
| **Enemies** (any living creature that is not an ally) | Wither I, Slowness I and 10 Darkness damage | Wither II, Slowness II and 20 Darkness damage |
| **Allies** (puppets, pets, subordinates and teammates) | Regeneration I and Speed I | Regeneration II and Speed II |

The effects last 3 seconds and are refreshed every 2 seconds while the target stays in the moss. **You do not receive the ally buffs yourself.** The damage is Darkness elemental damage.

| Cost and cooldown | Normal | Mastered |
|---|---|---|
| Magicule cost | 30,000 (paid on activation) | 40,000 (paid on activation) |
| Cooldown | 80 seconds | 60 seconds |
| Mastery | +1 per activation | — |

The cooldown only starts **when the skill ends**, either when you turn it off or when its duration runs out. No cooldown runs while it is active.

See [Twilight Moss](Twilight-Moss.md) for full details.

## Strategy

- **Fight with your puppets.** Twilight Moss buffs your allies with Regeneration and Speed while weakening enemies, so it pairs well with puppets from [Spore Puppeteer](Spore-Puppeteer.md).
- **Keep moving.** The trail keeps the moss active where you just were, so circling around enemies covers more ground than standing still.
- **Combine with Mycelial Reprisal.** Root yourself in the middle of the moss: enemies take Darkness damage and Wither while they hit you, and the stored energy finishes them with the shockwave.
- **Turn it off when the fight ends.** The cooldown only starts once the skill ends, so ending it early gets it ready sooner.
- **Plan your awakening.** The final evolution requires the True Demon Lord awakening, so prepare for it while gathering 2,000,000 EP.
