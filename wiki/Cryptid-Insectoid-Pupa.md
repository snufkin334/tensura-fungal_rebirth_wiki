# Cryptid Insectoid Pupa

The **Cryptid Insectoid Pupa** is a food item added by Fungal Rebirth. It is the key item for the first evolution of the [Fungal Lineage](Fungal-Lineage.md): a [Myconid Sprout](Myconid-Sprout.md) must eat 3 of them to evolve into a [Cordyceptic Stalker](Cordyceptic-Stalker.md).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Item ID** | `fungalrebirth:cryptid_insectoid_pupa` |
| **Type** | Food |
| **Stackable** | Yes (64) |
| **Creative tab** | Food & Drinks |
| **Renewable** | Yes |

## Obtaining

| Source | Chance | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Breaking a **Brown Mushroom** in the Ancient Forest | 0.8% | 1 | Only the small brown mushroom (`minecraft:brown_mushroom`). It must be broken by a player inside the `tensura:ancient_forest` biome. The pupa **replaces** the mushroom drop. |
| Breaking a [Rotten Oak Log](Rotten-Oak-Log.md) | 25% | 1 | Without Silk Touch. The log itself is not dropped. An explosion can destroy the log without dropping anything. |
| [Pile of Rotten Logs](Pile-of-Rotten-Logs.md) chest | 4% per chest | 1 | Separate roll from the rest of the chest loot |

> **Tip:** Rotten Oak Logs only appear in the [Pile of Rotten Logs](Pile-of-Rotten-Logs.md), so exploring the Ancient Forest for these structures is the fastest way to collect pupae.

## Usage

### Evolution

| Evolution | Amount |
|---|---|
| [Myconid Sprout](Myconid-Sprout.md) → [Cordyceptic Stalker](Cordyceptic-Stalker.md) | Consume 3 (worth 50% of the evolution progress) |

### Food

The pupa can be eaten like any other food.

---

## Food Properties

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Hunger restored | 2 (1 drumstick) |
| Saturation modifier | 0.1 |
| Can always be eaten | Yes, even with a full hunger bar |

### Effects when eaten

| Effect | Duration | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Nausea | 10 seconds | 100% |
| Poison I | 5 seconds | 100% |

> **Tip:** Because it can always be eaten, you can consume all 3 pupae for the evolution at once, even when you are not hungry.
