# Tensura: Fungal Rebirth

**Fungal Rebirth** is an addon for [Tensura: Reincarnated](https://tensura.wiki.gg/) on **NeoForge 1.21.1**. It adds a new evolutionary lineage of mushroom races, born in the **Ancient Forest**. You start as a fragile sprout and can grow into a primordial mycelial seraph.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Author** | nvkxng |
| **Mod ID** | `fungalrebirth` |
| **Loader** | NeoForge (Minecraft 1.21.1) |
| **Parent mod** | Tensura: Reincarnated |

The lineage has **5 races**. Each race grants a unique **Intrinsic Skill**, and the skills of earlier races are kept as you evolve. The addon also adds a new item, new blocks, two status effects and a new world structure in the Ancient Forest.

> **A growing project:** Fungal Rebirth is a small project by **nvkxng**, still in active development. Future updates are expected to add new creatures, new structures and other evolutionary lines. This wiki will be updated as new content is released.

## Features at a Glance

- **A new starting race.** [Myconid Sprout](Myconid-Sprout.md) is added automatically to Tensura's starting race selection. When you become a Myconid Sprout, you are teleported near the **Labyrinth** tree in the Ancient Forest and your spawn point is set there. See [Getting Started](Getting-Started.md).
- **A 5-phase evolution line.** Evolve from [Myconid Sprout](Myconid-Sprout.md) to [Genesis Mycoseraph](Genesis-Mycoseraph.md). The final race is classified as both **Divine** and **Spiritual**. See [Fungal Lineage](Fungal-Lineage.md).
- **Magicule Poison immunity.** Every fungal race is immune to Magicule Poison, at all times.
- **Five Intrinsic Skills.** [Fungal Anatomy](Fungal-Anatomy.md), [Spore Puppeteer](Spore-Puppeteer.md), [Mycelial Reprisal](Mycelial-Reprisal.md), [Twilight Moss](Twilight-Moss.md) and [Perennial Rebirth](Perennial-Rebirth.md).
- **Racial mechanics.**
  - [Sprout Stealth](Sprout-Stealth.md): the first two races hide inside tall grass and large ferns.
  - [Spore Deprivation](Spore-Deprivation.md): a Myconid Sprout weakens when it leaves the Ancient Forest.
- **New content in the Ancient Forest.**
  - The [Pile of Rotten Logs](Pile-of-Rotten-Logs.md) structure, with loot chests and Phantaspores.
  - The [Rotten Oak Log](Rotten-Oak-Log.md) block.
  - The [Cryptid Insectoid Pupa](Cryptid-Insectoid-Pupa.md), the item needed for the first evolution.
